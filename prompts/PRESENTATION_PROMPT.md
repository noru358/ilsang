# PRESENTATION_PROMPT

Create the quality-first `PRESENTATION_MASTER_DRAFT` for an approved ilsang episode.

Inputs:

- accepted COVER artwork
- accepted extracted BODY artwork S01–S06
- approved literal copy
- approved storyboard semantics

## Purpose

Design the seven-page carousel as a human-authored comic presentation before editable reconstruction.

You may make organic presentation decisions about:

- speech/thought/narration/SFX hierarchy
- bubble silhouette and tail character
- explicit line breaks
- typography character
- scale/rotation/emphasis
- cover title composition
- placement around focal faces/hands/action

Do not alter the approved artwork identity.

Do not invent new copy merely to fit a layout.

Do not simplify presentation just because ToonDesk has generic defaults; the presentation master is the upstream visual target.

Output the final visual preview for the user's publish approval.
